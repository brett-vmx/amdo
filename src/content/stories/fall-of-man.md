The man and woman lived in the garden God made. In this garden there were many delicious fruits. Not only this, but there was also a tree to know the difference between good and evil.

God told the man and woman, “You can eat any fruit in the garden. But do not eat the fruit on the tree to know the difference between good and evil. If you eat it, you will die.”

One day a snake came to the woman and said, “Did God say you cannot eat any fruit in the garden?” The woman said, “God told us we can eat the fruit of any tree, but we cannot eat the fruit upon the tree to know the difference between good and evil. If we eat it, we will die.”

The snake said, “You will not die. If you eat this fruit, you will become like God. This is why God said not to eat this fruit.”

Then the woman thought about what the snake said, and she wanted to be a wise person. So, she ate the fruit, and she gave some to the man beside her, and he also ate.

Now, before they were naked. But they didn’t know they were naked. After they ate the fruit, they knew they were naked and were embarrassed.

Some time later God came into the garden. The man and the woman were afraid and hid among the trees. Then God asked, “Man, where are you?” Then the man told God everything that they had done, but the man put all the blame on the woman. And the woman also put all the blame on the snake.

Then God punished them and said, “One day you will die.” And God kicked the man and woman out of the garden, and they could never go back into the garden. From then on, there was sin in the world, and there was also suffering in the world. But, God had compassion on them and helped them in their lives.
