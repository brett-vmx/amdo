Through the man and the woman, there became many more people. But, most people sinned and did not have faith in/worship God.

But God still had compassion on all people. Therefore, in order to save them from their sins God first chose a man named Abraham. He was a nomad.

God gave Abraham many blessings. Among these, one amazing blessing was the one giving him a son. If one asks “How did He give this son?”

One day when Abraham was 100 years old, God came into Abraham’s tent. Abraham’s wife prepared bread, yoghurt, milk, and meat and offered them up to Him. Then God said this, “Abraham, I am going to give you a son,” He said this.

Because Abraham’s wife was very old, she said, “This isn’t true. How can this be true?” she said and laughed. God said to Abraham, “What is the reason your wife is laughing? There is no work I cannot do,” He said. Now after this, indeed Abraham’s wife had a son.

Through Abraham’s descendants, in the future a big Savior will be born. This big Savior will save all people from sin.
