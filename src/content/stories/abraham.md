Through the man and the woman, there became more and more people in the world. But, most people sinned and did not worship God.

But God still had compassion on all people. Therefore, in order to save them from their sins, God chose a man named Abraham. He was a nomad.

God blessed Abraham very much. Among these, one amazing blessing was giving him a son. If one asks, “How did He give this son?”

When Abraham was 100 years old, God came to Abraham’s tent. Abraham’s wife prepared bread, yoghurt, milk, and meat and gave them to Him. Then God said this, “Abraham, I am going to give you a son.”

Abraham’s wife was very old, and she laughed and said, “This isn’t true. How can this be true?” God said to Abraham, “Why is your wife laughing? There is nothing I cannot do.” And after this, Abraham’s wife indeed had a son.

Now, in the future, through Abraham’s descendants, a great Savior will be born. This great Savior will save all people from sin.
