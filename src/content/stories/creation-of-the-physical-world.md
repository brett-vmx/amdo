In the beginning, there was only God in the world. God made the world(sky-earth), land, mountains, water, and the various kinds of animals. God also made a man.

If it is said, "How did God make people?" (He) took the dust of the earth, made the form/image of a person, He breathed life into the person’s nose, then the person became alive.

God also made a garden. In the garden, there was a river and different kinds of trees, not only this, but many delicious fruit on the trees.

God made that person live in the garden, and had (him) take care of the garden. Then that person took care of the garden, and did farm work in the garden.

One day, God brought all the various kinds of animals before that person, and had that person name them. That person gave names to every animal. Then every animal had its own name.

Every animal had its own living companion and going companion. But the person was alone. So God put that person into a deep sleep, and took a rib from that person, and made a woman. Then God brought the woman before the person, and the person was very happy, “Wow, this is really like me.” (he) said.

Then God blessed them man and the woman and put them in charge over all that He made. God looked at all He made, and He thought it was very good. At this time there was not any sin, there was also not any suffering. Everything was very good.
