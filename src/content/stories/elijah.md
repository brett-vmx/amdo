From among Abraham's descendants, there was an evil king. That king worshiped idols, and his people also worshiped both God and idols. Therefore, God was not happy.

God sent a person named Elijah. This person called the people and the mystics up to the top of a mountain. He called them up to the top of a mountain and said, "Why do you worship both God and idols? It is right for us to only worship whoever is the true one."

Then Elijah also said, "Today let's do a contest. You make an altar and prepare an offering. Then, pray to your gods. I will pray to my own God. Whoever can make fire burn the offering, that is the true one."

Then the mystics prayed to their gods. They prayed for many hours, but not a single sign occurred. And Elijah joked, "Does your god have no free time today? Or has he gone somewhere? Or is he sleeping?"

The mystics prayed very loudly. They prayed over and over, but still not a single sign happened. Then Elijah made an altar and poured lots of water on it. He prayed like this, "God, today, show that You are the true one."

Suddenly, a great fire came down from the sky and burned the altar into dust and ashes. There was water on the altar, and they saw the fire burning even in the water, and everyone was amazed. Therefore, the people changed their hearts and worshiped God.
