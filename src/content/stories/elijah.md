From among Abraham's descendants, there was an evil king. That king had faith in idols. His people also had faith in both God and idols. Therefore, God was unhappy.

God sent a person called Elijah. This person called the people and the mystics up to the top of a mountain. He called them up to the top of a mountain, he said this, "Why do you have faith in both God and idols? Whoever is the true one, it is right for us to have faith in that one." (He) said like that.

Then Elijah also said, "Today let's do a test. You make an altar, and prepare an offering. Then, you pray to your god. I will pray to my own God. Whoever is able to make fire burn the offering, that is the true one." (He) said like that.

Then the mystics prayed to their god. They prayed for many hours but a single sign did not even occur. Elijah (said), "Your god has no free time today? Or has he gone somewhere? Is he sleeping?" He joked like that.

The mystics prayed very loudly. They prayed over and over. Still not a single sign happened. Then Elijah made an altar and poured lots of water on it. He prayed like this, "God, today, show that You are the true one." (He) prayed like that.

Suddenly a great fire came down from the sky and, made the altar into ashes and dust. There was water on the altar. They saw a fire was made in the water, and everyone was amazed. Therefore, the people changed their hearts, and they had faith in God.
