The place where Abraham’s descendants lived had a famine. So, they left and went to another country, and they lived in that country for many years. But, the people of that country were afraid of Abraham’s descendants and enslaved them.

So, their lives became more and more difficult. And they prayed to God many times, "Help us!" God heard their request and had compassion on them. So, God chose a person named Moses from among Abraham's descendants to save them from that country.

Then God said to Moses, "Go and tell the king, 'Let Abraham’s descendants go!" So, Moses and his older brother went and told the king.

But that king said, "Who is this one called 'God'? I don't know God at all. I won't let Abraham’s descendants go." He not only said this, but he also said, "But show me an amazing sign."

Moses' older brother threw the stick in his hand on the ground, and the stick turned into a snake. Then the king summoned his own country's sorcerers. They threw their own sticks on the ground, and they also turned into snakes. Moses' brother's snake that had miraculously transformed ate all the magicians' snakes. But the king still said, "I won't let Abraham’s descendants go."

Therefore, God, over and over, sent many destructions. After many destructions came, the king said, "You all don’t stay here! Go!"

Then Abraham's descendants were free from the power of that country, and they returned to their own land.
