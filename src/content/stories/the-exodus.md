The place that Abraham’s descendants had, had a famine. Therefore, they left from that land and went to another country. They lived in that country for many years. The people of that country were afraid of Abraham’s descendants, and made them slaves.

Then their lives got harder and harder. They (requested to) God, "Help us!" This they requested many times. God, hearing their request, had compassion on them. In order to save them from that country, God chose a person named Moses from among Abraham's descendants.

Then God (said) to Moses, "You go and (say) to the king, 'Let Abraham’s descendants go!' Say that." He said that. Moses and his older brother went and said it like that to the king.

But that king (said), "Who is what (you) are calling 'God'? I don't know God at all. I won't let Abraham’s descendants go." (He) not only said that, but also, "But show me an amazing sign." He said that.

Moses' older brother threw the stick in his hand down on the ground, this stick turned into a snake. The king also summoned his own country's magicians/sorcerers. They threw their own sticks on the ground, they also turned into snakes. Moses' brother's snake that had miraculously transformed ate all the magicians' snakes. But the king still said, "I won't let Abraham’s descendants go." He said that.

Therefore God, over and over made many destruction (plagues) come. After many destructions/plagues came, the king said, "You all don’t stay here! Go!" he said that. Then Abraham's descendants were free from under the power of that country, (and) they went back to their own land.
