One day, Savior Jesus and his students were going together to another place, and on the road they met a blind man. Savior Jesus' students asked him: "Why can't this person see? Is it because his father and mother sinned? Or because he himself sinned?"

Savior Jesus replied, "It is not because he sinned, and it is also not because his father and mother sinned. Today, God is going to do something amazing in his life.

Savior Jesus spat on the ground, prepared a small piece of mud, and rubbed it in the eyes of the blind man. Then He said to him, "Go to the edge of the pond and wash your eyes." So, the blind man washed his eyes off at the edge of the pond, and he could see clearly. Then he went back.

The blind man returned back to his hometown and this place’s religious leaders asked him, "How were your eyes healed?" He replied, "Savior Jesus healed them." But the religious leaders wouldn’t listen to any of it. They didn’t worship Savior Jesus, and they didn’t believe this person either.

The religious leaders brought his parents here and asked them if this matter was true or not. The man’s parents were very afraid of the religious leaders. There was a law to kick anyone out of the religious house who had faith in Savior Jesus. For that reason, they wouldn’t say that Savior Jesus had made their own son’s eyes well.

The religious leaders again called this guy and asked him. They said, "Tell us the truth. Who really healed your eyes?" He replied, "Savior Jesus made my eyes well. I think God sent Him." Then the religious leaders were very unhappy. They mocked him and threw him out of the religious house.

Savior Jesus heard about this whole matter, and Savior Jesus met the man. He asked him, "Do you have faith in that Savior that God sent?" The man replied, "Who is He? If I knew, I would have faith in Him. Tell me." Then Savior Jesus said, "That person is me." Then that man put his faith in Savior Jesus and praised God.
