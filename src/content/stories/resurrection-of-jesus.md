After Savior Jesus died upon a wooden cross, other people buried His body in a tomb. Three days later, some women who had faith in Savior Jesus went to where His tomb was. Then, when they arrived at the place where the tomb was and took a look around, not only had the tomb's entrance been opened, Savior Jesus' body was no longer there. They became surprised. At that time 2 angels arrived, "Because Savior Jesus has become alive again, He is not here." Like that said.

Then those women became happy, and ran off and told Savior Jesus' students this situation/news. The students thought that the women had gone crazy. What they say isn’t true. How can it be true, they thought.

That evening, as Savior Jesus' students and some (others) who had faith in Him were gathered together, Saviour Jesus suddenly arrived among them. They were afraid and surprised. Savior Jesus (said), "You don't need to be afraid. Look at me. It's me." He said like that. And when they saw that Savior Jesus had really come back to life, and they became very happy.

Then He, (told them) why He needed to be killed, and about why He needed to be made alive again, (He) clearly told them.

If one asks why, some of Jesus’ students (announced), “Whoever has sin, God must punish that person. But Savior Jesus in place of all people, and for the sake of cleansing people's sins, (He) died upon a wooden cross.

Whoever changes their heart (repents), and has faith in Savior Jesus alone, that person's sins are not only cleansed, he will be saved/delivered. That is the good news about Jesus.” Like that announced.
